Popular Destinations

Tinago Falls

Tinago Falls is a waterfall hidden in a deep gorge with a bright blue-green pool. You need to walk down many steps to reach it. It is a great place for swimming and taking photos.

Location: Iligan City, Lanao del Norte

Sunken Cemetery

The Sunken Cemetery is marked by a big white cross standing in the sea. It sank after a volcanic eruption long ago. Visitors can go by boat or snorkel around it.

Location: Catarman, Camiguin

White Island

White Island is a long white sandbar in the middle of clear blue water. There are no trees, so bring a hat and sunscreen. It has a nice view of Mt. Hibok-Hibok.

Location: Mambajao, Camiguin

Bukidnon Highlands

Bukidnon has rolling green hills and cool weather. It is a good place for road trips, farm visits, and nature viewing.

Location: Bukidnon Province

Cagayan de Oro City

Cagayan de Oro is the busiest city in the region. It has malls, a seaport, and restaurants. It is also called the City of Golden Friendship.

Location: Cagayan de Oro, Misamis Oriental
