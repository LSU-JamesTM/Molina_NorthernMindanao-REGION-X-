Travel Tips

  • The dry months are the best time to visit.
  • Bring cash because some places have no ATM.
  • Book boat trips early during holidays.
  • Respect local rules and keep places clean.

Packing Checklist

  • Sunscreen and hat
  • Swimsuit and extra clothes
  • Comfortable shoes or sandals
  • Raincoat or umbrella
  • Water bottle
  • Phone charger / power bank
