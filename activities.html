Activities & Experiences

Adventure

  • Whitewater rafting on the Cagayan de Oro River
  • Ziplining and adventure rides at Dahilayan, Bukidnon
  • Hiking the trails of Camiguin

Nature & Water

  • Snorkeling in the clear waters of Camiguin
  • Swimming at Tinago Falls
  • Island hopping to White Island

Food

  • Try kinilaw (fresh fish in vinegar) in Misamis Oriental
  • Eat pastel, the soft sweet bread of Camiguin
  • Taste fresh pineapple and lanzones

Culture

  • Join a local festival
  • Visit old churches and heritage spots
  • Learn about the Higaonon and Talaandig tribes in Bukidnon
