Welcome to Region X

Northern Mindanao, also known as Region X, is found in the northern part of Mindanao in the Philippines. It is made up of five provinces: Bukidnon, Camiguin, Lanao del Norte, Misamis Occidental, and Misamis Oriental. The regional center is Cagayan de Oro City.

The region is known for its waterfalls, white sandbars, green highlands, and exciting outdoor adventures. Visitors can enjoy whitewater rafting, ziplining, island hopping, and delicious local food. Explore this website to plan your next trip!
