Our History & Culture

Brief History

Long before the Spanish came, the people of Northern Mindanao lived in communities near rivers and coasts. Indigenous groups such as the Higaonon and Talaandig lived in the highlands of Bukidnon and kept their own traditions.

During the Spanish and American periods, the region became more connected through trade, farming, and the building of towns and ports. Today, Region X is one of the growing areas of Mindanao, and Cagayan de Oro serves as its center for business and education.

Cultural Highlights

Festivals

  • Kaamulan Festival (Bukidnon)
  • Lanzones Festival (Camiguin)
  • Higalaay Festival (Cagayan de Oro)
  • Kaliga-on Festival (Iligan City)

Traditional Crafts and Foods

  • Higaonon and Talaandig woven crafts
  • Kinilaw
  • Pastel of Camiguin
  • Muscovado sugar and pineapple products of Bukidnon
